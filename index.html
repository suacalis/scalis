About Me

Prof. Dr. Şuayyip Çalış is a Professor at Sakarya University's Faculty of Business, Department of Human Resources Management. With extensive academic experience and expertise in human resources management, labor economics, and industrial relations, he has made significant contributions to the field of business education and research.

His research interests include compliance, empirical legal analysis, machine learning applications in business, and the European Court of Human Rights jurisprudence. Committed to education and sustainable development goals, Prof. Çalış focuses on quality research and student mentorship at Sakarya University.
